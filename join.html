Before you join

The request form is free. Individual hikes may have separate registration, transport, entrance, or guide costs.

Do I need hiking experience?
No. Hike listings explain difficulty and preparation so you can choose an appropriate outing.
Does this book me onto a hike?
No. Joining gets you community updates. Open hikes use their own registration path.
When should I hear back?
Allow up to two business days. If you do not hear back, email {{ site.email }}.
